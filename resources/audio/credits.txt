Quebble audio credits

Music: Title Screen, Juhani Junkala / SubspaceAudio
Source collection: Retro Game Music Pack / 5 Chiptunes (Action)
https://opengameart.org/content/5-chiptunes-action
Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/5%20Action%20Chiptunes%20By%20Juhani%20Junkala.zip
Adaptation: complete 11.294-second loop, mono 8 kHz signed 16-bit PCM,
420 Hz high-pass, presence EQ, normalization and 2 ms edge fades.

Effects: Kenney / Kenney.nl, Digital SFX Set (2012)
https://opengameart.org/content/63-digital-sound-effects-lasers-phasers-space-etc
Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Digital_SFX_Set.zip
Adaptations: shortened, filtered, normalized, mono 8 kHz signed 16-bit PCM;
soft edge fades and silent effect handles, for clean starts and decays;
lowDown is also pitch-shifted for the game-over cue.
UI/countdown: pepSound3; start: powerUp2; claim: powerUp7;
hit/game over: lowDown; clear: highUp.
